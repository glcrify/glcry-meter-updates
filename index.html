FAQ

Will every shot be green?

No tool can honestly promise that. The game reads your controller once per frame and online play adds delay. GLCRY METER releases within a few milliseconds of where you set it, so once your timing is dialled in, it's as consistent as your connection allows.

How do I set my timing?

Open Shot settings in GLCRY METER. If your shots come out early, press -; if they're late, press +. The change takes effect on your next shot and stays where you put it.

Which modes does it work with?

NBA 2K21 on PC, on our community servers, offline and online. Online play usually needs a little more timing because of server delay.

Why does Windows ask for permission when I open it?

GLCRY METER hides your real controller from the game so 2K only sees one controller. Windows asks for permission to do that each time you open it. Press Yes.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your Stripe receipt email. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

Can I use it on two PCs?

Each seat is one PC. If you change PCs, message us on Discord and we'll move your key.

What if it doesn't work on my PC?

Message us on Discord first. Most problems are a quick settings fix. If we can't get it running, we refund your first month.

Where can I see it in action?

Clips go up on TikTok at @glcrify, and the community is on discord.gg/glcrify.

What do you do with my details?

See Terms & privacy. In short: your Discord name and email let us support you, and your PC's ID locks your key to it. We never sell your data.

Setup guide

  1. Run the installerPress Download GLCRY METER above. If your browser asks, choose Keep. Double-click GLCRY METER Setup and press Yes. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. That's normal for new apps.
  2. Let it finishA window installs everything for you in about 5 minutes, OBS Studio included. Seven steps tick off, then GLCRY METER opens by itself. If the installer says restart your PC, do that before your first game. It only happens the first time.
  3. Paste your keyCopy your key from this page and paste it into GLCRY METER. You'll see "Activating this PC", then the main screen. Your key now belongs to this PC.
  4. Start NBA 2K21OBS is installed and set up for you (1920x1080, 240 fps, a capture of NBA 2K21), and GLCRY METER opens it with the Virtual Camera already running. Just start the game; GLCRY METER's VIDEO card turns green. Already used OBS before? Your own scenes are left alone, so add a Game Capture of NBA 2K21, set Settings → Video to 1920x1080 and Integer FPS value 240, and press Start Virtual Camera.
  5. Choose your controllerGLCRY METER asks how your controller is connected. Pick it. From then on, Windows asks for permission each time you open GLCRY METER; press Yes, as that's how it hides your real controller from the game. If 2K21 was already open, unplug your controller and plug it back in once. The CONTROLLER card turns green when it's ready.
  6. Set your timing and playTake a few shots. If they're consistently early, open Shot settings and press -; if late, press +. Small steps work best. Updates install themselves every time you open GLCRY METER.

If something's not right

The game sees two controllers, or reacts twice to one press

Close GLCRY METER, unplug your controller, plug it back in and open GLCRY METER again (press Yes on the Windows prompt). If it still happens, restart your PC once.

My controller doesn't move in the game

Close GLCRY METER with the X so it gives the game your controller back. If you have the HidHide window open, close it too. Then open GLCRY METER again and press Yes. Still stuck? Unplug and replug your controller.

The VIDEO card stays amber

Make sure NBA 2K21 is running. OBS sits in the system tray (bottom-right, by the clock); open it and check Start Virtual Camera is on and the game shows in the preview. If the preview is black, double-click NBA 2K21 in Sources and pick the game window. In GLCRY METER's Advanced view, pick OBS Virtual Camera as the capture device.

The VIDEO card says it's below 100 fps

Set OBS to 240 fps (Settings → Video → FPS → Integer FPS value) and make sure the game itself runs above that or close to it. Close other recording software while you play.
