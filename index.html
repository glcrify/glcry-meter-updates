FAQ

Will every shot be EXCELLENT?

No tool can promise that. The game reads your controller once per frame and online play adds delay. GLCRY METER releases within a few milliseconds of the middle of the window and corrects itself from 2K's TIMING result, so most of your shots land in the window.

Which games and modes does it work with?

NBA 2K21 on PC, on our community servers. It handles offline modes and online modes like Ante-Up. Online play needs more lead time because of server delay, and GLCRY METER re-tunes itself from 2K's TIMING result after a few shots.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your Stripe receipt email. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

Can I use it on two PCs?

Each seat is one PC. If you change PCs, message us on Discord and we'll move your key.

What if it doesn't work on my PC?

Message us on Discord first. Most problems are a quick settings fix. If we can't get it running, we refund your first month.

Where can I see it in action?

Clips go up on TikTok at @glcrify, and the community is on discord.gg/glcrify.

What do you do with my details?

See Terms & privacy. In short: your Discord name and email let us support you, and your PC ID locks your key. We never sell your data.
